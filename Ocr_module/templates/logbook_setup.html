Set up a logbook

Do this once for each kind of logbook. After that, every new scan of that logbook is read by itself: you do not need to do this again for new scans.

    Step 1: Choose a scan of the logbook

    Use a clear, straight scan of one open logbook, with the printed column names at the top showing. Best is a PDF with the left page first, then the right page. A photo of one page also works.

    Looking at the pages and finding the columns. This takes up to a minute…

    What is set up now

      What to do on this page
      1. Look at the picture. Each coloured stripe is one column of the logbook, with its number and name at the top. Each patient should sit inside one dashed box.
      2. If every stripe sits on the right column, press Yes, this page is right.
      3. A black dividing line in the wrong place? Press on it (or on its round handle) and drag it onto the printed line. It jumps onto the line when you let go.
      4. A wrong name? Change it in the list under the picture.
